  • February 1975

    Grand Opening

    Awesome Bowling Alley opened for the very first time on June 10th, 1975. Our establishment was completely family owned and operated, and quickly became a popular local spot.

  • November 1981

    Second Location Opens

    After an unexpected boom in popularity, there was demand for a new location in a more populated area. On November 20th, 1981, a second location opened in Phoenix, AZ!

  • Oct 1986

    Paul Simon Celebrates Here

    On October 13th, 1986, famous singer-songwriter Paul Simon celebrated his birthday at Awesome Bowling Alley. Ex-partner Art Garfunkel did not attend.

  • August 1990

    Our First Location in California

    After years of support from Arizona's dedicated bowlers, Awesome Bowling Alley was able to expand into California.

  • January 1998

    Featured in Film

    Awesome Bowling Alley was featured in the hit 1998 Coen Brothers comedy The Big Lebowski. Good movie.

  • May 2000

    Kitchen Added

    Rennovations at the Phoenix location allowed for the first Awesome Bowling Alley kitchen to open, providing delicious food options to bowlers, including the famous Awesome burger.
